# Conflict resolution

Inspect status and the operation in progress. Read both sides and surrounding
callers/tests; resolve intended behavior, not just markers. Preserve useful tests
from both branches. Ask about incompatible requirements rather than choosing a
side arbitrarily. Do not abort another person's operation or rewrite published
history without authority.

Verify the integrated result with relevant tests and lint; use behavioral
regressions for changed behavior. Inspect staged paths safely and check remaining
markers. Continue only the authorized operation with required signing and hooks.
Inspect HEAD/status before retrying ambiguous failures.
